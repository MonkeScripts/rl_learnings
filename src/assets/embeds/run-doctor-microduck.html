Microduck Run Doctor

A diagnostic and replay layer for sim2real PPO runs. It reads the config that produced a run, predicts what the curves should do, flags only what they shouldn't, and lets you stand in the sim at the exact iteration a flag fired.

run 2026-09-14_17-56-13_velocity 50,000 iterations 4,096 envs 200 checkpoints curriculum done by it 2,000
The gap

Every tracker sees anonymous scalars. The config that produced them sits unread beside the log.

Reading a run well is a six-step loop. Weights & Biases, Comet, Neptune and TensorBoard cover exactly one of the six. Roboto's tag-and-summarize covers a coarse version of the same one. The other five are what an experienced engineer does in their head, and all five are cheap to automate because the inputs already exist on disk: params/env.yaml holds every curriculum boundary, penalty name and target value; the tfevents file holds every curve.

1
Context
What changed since the last run? Diff two env.yaml files.
2
Expect
Given the change and the curriculum schedule, what should the curves do, and when?
3
Observe
What did the curves actually do?
4
Compare
Is the delta between expected and observed concerning, or a scheduled shock?
5
Diagnose
Group co-timed symptoms under one root cause. Disambiguate with a second metric.
6
Decide
Wait, stop, or change one thing for the next run. Replay the moment to be sure.

Five things nothing does today

  1. Curriculum-aware expectationenv.yaml says a shock lands at step 12,000. Predict the dip, open a recovery window, flag only non-recovery.
  2. Reward-semantics checksPenalty sign audit. Task term flat while total rises. Weighted Episode_Reward/* reconciled against raw Metrics/*. Inert terms.
  3. Config diff → hypothesis → testDiff two runs' env.yaml, attach an expected effect to each change, check the curves for it. Comet diffs configs; nobody reasons about them.
  4. Phase-conditioned thresholdsσ, entropy and learning rate judged against the phase the run is in, not a global constant.
  5. Incident → checkpoint replayA flag at iteration k links to model_k.pt, the curriculum state at k, and a recorded rollout window you can scrub.
Design

Deterministic first, replay on flag, narrator last.

The value is in the flags that don't fire. A tool that alerts on every dip is muted within a day. The expectation model exists to suppress the six scheduled shocks in this run and leave attention for the seventh thing, whatever it is.
Replay

Standing in the sim at iteration k means rewinding the world, not just the weights.

Three components, all small

ComponentWhat it doesBuilds onSize
Counter rewindSet env.common_step_counter = k × 24 after load, invoke each curriculum term once so weights, ranges and DR match iteration k.mjlab play · CurriculumManager~20 lines
RecorderTermFor N tracked envs, log qpos, qvel, data.contact and per-env per-step reward terms to Rerun on a step timeline. Geometry via rerun-loader-mjcf.cfg.recorders · record_post_step~1 day
TriggerRecord only around curriculum boundaries and flagged windows, so the recorder is cheap enough to leave on during real training.rule engine events~50 lines
Per-env, per-step rewards are the hidden prerequisite. Every wandb metric is a mean over 4,096 envs. Reward hacking starts in the minority of envs with an extreme randomization draw before it moves the mean. A recorder that sees per-env terms catches "3% of envs have zero air time while tracking is perfect" hundreds of iterations early.
Real time

The engine belongs inside the training process. Knowing at minute 10 that a run is worth stopping is the whole payoff.

Every task in this repo is already registered with MicroduckOnPolicyRunner. rsl_rl's learn() calls self.logger.log(...) once per iteration with the same dict it sends to Weights & Biases, and the rollout loop already carries one in-process guard that halts training: check_for_nan. Wrapping that one log call gives the rule engine every scalar at iteration granularity with no API, no polling and no ingestion layer. The polling paths remain for runs you can't instrument, such as Hugging Face Jobs.

Advisor, not operator

The doctor never stops a run. An automatic kill is the one action where a false positive costs more than the bug it prevents, and the person watching has context the rules don't. Every event, including critical, posts and training continues. What the doctor owes you in return is an alert you can act on without opening a dashboard: what was observed, what it most likely means, and what your options are with their cost.

[warn] inert_term · it 512 · 10 min elapsed
  Episode_Reward/angular_momentum has read 0.000000 for 500 consecutive
  iterations while its weight is -0.02.
  Likely: robot/root_angmom sensor returns zeros, or the selector matches
  no body. The term is contributing nothing to training.
  Consider: this run is unaffected either way; fix before the next run.
  Replay: model_500.pt · uv run play Microduck-Velocity-v0 --checkpoint 500

Three parts, every time. The last two lines are what turn a flag into a decision you can make in ten seconds.

Why Weights & Biases hasn't done this. The platform sees anonymous scalars from every framework in existence. The rules on this page are legible only because the tool can read env.yaml and knows what action_rate_l2 means, that Curriculum/* is a schedule and not a metric, and which terms are penalties. That knowledge is per-codebase. A general tracker can offer z-scores; a doctor has to live in the repo.
Rules

A first ten, with thresholds taken from this run.

RuleConditionPhaseSeverityRecommended action
penalty_signany Episode_Reward/<penalty> > 0 at any stepallcriticalStop. Negative weight on a self-negating *_penalty. Fix sign, restart.
inert_term|Episode_Reward/x| < 1e-6 for 500 consecutive iterations while weight ≠ 0allwarningSensor or selector returns zeros. Verify the term computes on a smoke test.
nan_stateEpisode_Termination/nan_state > 0 in 3 of 10 iterationsallcriticalPhysics instability. Dump the offending env, check solver settings and DR extremes.
curriculum_shocktask or total reward drops >5% within 50 steps of an env.yaml boundaryhardeningsuppressedOpen a 200-iteration window. Escalate to no_recovery only if the raw metric behind the term stays below pre-shock.
no_recoveryraw Metrics/* for the shocked term still < pre-shock at window closehardeningwarningStage too aggressive. Split it or move it later, never earlier.
shuffletrack_linear_velocity rising and Metrics/air_time_mean < 0.05discovery+warningFeet not lifting. Raise air_time or foot_clearance weight; check the command gate.
premature_convergencePolicy/mean_std < 0.3 before σ-phase 2 (here: it 791)explorationwarningExploration collapsed. Raise entropy_coef or init_std; check for a farmable term.
window_edgea gated metric sits within 5% of its reward window edge for 2,000 iterationsconsolidationinfoPolicy is taking the minimum that pays (air time at 0.1254 vs floor 0.125). Decide if that gait is what you want; if not, move the floor.
total_up_task_flatTrain/mean_reward rising over 1,000 iterations while every task term's slope ≤ 0allwarningReward rising on regularizers alone. Check episode length: 20 s means standing still and surviving.
lr_floorLoss/learning_rate at 1e-5 for 300 iterationsallwarningEvery update overshoots KL 0.01. Look for a reward cliff: a std that's too tight or a binary term.

Applied to this run: penalty_sign passes, inert_term fires on angular_momentum, curriculum_shock suppresses six alerts, window_edge fires on air time, premature_convergence does not fire (σ crossed 0.3 at it 791, after walking was established at it ~90).

Roadmap

Each level is useful on its own and feeds the next.

Level 1

Curriculum-aware alerts

  • Rule engine wrapped around logger.log in MicroduckOnPolicyRunner; W&B polling as the remote fallback
  • Reads env.yaml for boundaries, penalty set, windows
  • Runs the ten rules above with suppression windows
  • Posts typed events to Slack: observed, likely cause, options, replay command
days · a failing run costs 10 minutes, not 16 hours
Level 2

Run analyzer and replay

  • Post-hoc report: phases, incidents, grouped root causes
  • env.yaml diff against the previous run with expected effects
  • Counter rewind in play; RecorderTerm to Rerun
  • Click a flag, scrub the rollout at that iteration
weeks · closes the curve-to-sim loop
Level 3

Training copilot

  • Live phase state machine with causal grouping
  • Per-env reward monitoring for early hack detection
  • Narrator proposes one change from events + diff + replay stats
  • Optional: hands the proposal to mjlab grid-search
months · the recommendation layer
